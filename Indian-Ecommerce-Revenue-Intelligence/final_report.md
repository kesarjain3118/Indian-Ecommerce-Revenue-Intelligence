## Methodology

### 1. RFM Analysis
- **Approach**: Calculated Recency (days since last purchase), Frequency (# orders), Monetary (total revenue) for each customer
- **Scoring**: Divided customers into quintiles (1-5) for each metric
- **Segmentation**: Created 9 distinct customer segments based on RFM score combinations

### 2. Cohort Retention
- **Cohort Definition**: Monthly signup cohorts (24 total from Jan 2023 - Dec 2024)
- **Retention Calculation**: % of cohort making purchases in each subsequent month
- **Tracking Period**: Up to 23 months post-signup

### 3. Customer Lifetime Value
- **Historical CLV**: Sum of all revenue generated by each customer
- **Segmentation**: Divided into quintiles (Very Low to Very High)
- **Channel Analysis**: Compared average CLV across 6 acquisition channels

### 4. Product Affinity
- **Algorithm**: Apriori market basket analysis
- **Metrics**: Support, confidence, and lift for product associations
- **Threshold**: Minimum 5 co-occurrences (0.5% support)

### 5. Seasonal Analysis
- **Time Periods**: Monthly, yearly, day-of-week
- **Metrics**: Order volume, revenue, average order value
- **Comparisons**: Year-over-year growth rates

### 6. Churn Prediction
- **Definition**: No purchase in 90+ days
- **Model**: Random Forest classifier with 7 features
- **Performance**: 100% accuracy, 1.000 ROC-AUC score
